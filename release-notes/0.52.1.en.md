Nota 0.52 is about getting to the music faster: a snap grid that follows your zoom, new projects that are ready to play, and the mix levels right in the arrangement. Plus a long list of fixes, including a loud reverb glitch and a crash with plug-ins like Reaktor.

**1. A snap grid that follows the zoom**
The arrangement, the piano roll and the audio clip editor now snap to the grid you see. In **Auto** (the new default) zoom in for sixteenths, zoom out and clips move by whole bars; choose Narrow, Medium or Wide density, or pick a fixed step from 1/32 to 4 bars. Triplets work everywhere. The GRID cell and the new grid chip open a menu with every option, and the keyboard covers the rest: **⌘1 / ⌘2** narrower / wider, **⌘3** triplets, **⌘4** snap on / off, **⌘5** Auto ↔ fixed (Ctrl on Windows and Linux). In the audio clip editor, trim brackets, warp markers and envelope points snap too.

**2. New projects come ready to play**
A new project starts with an instrument track, an audio track and two returns: **Reverb** and **Delay**, both set to Wet Only. Turn up a send and you hear space or echoes straight away. They are ordinary tracks, so edit or delete them as you like.

**3. Levels and volume on Return and Master**
The Return and Master rows at the bottom of the arrangement now have a level meter and a volume fader with its dB value. Watch a return light up as you turn up a send, or pull down the whole mix without opening the Mixer. Double-click a fader to reset it to 0 dB. The Master volume is now one value shared by the Mixer, the transport and the arrangement, and it's saved with the project.

**4. Long audio files stay smooth**
Dropping a multi-minute file no longer stalls the app: time-stretching runs in the background while the track says "Processing…". Tempo, marker and warp edits in the clip editor work the same way, and the clip keeps playing until the new version is ready. The clip editor's waveform now sharpens as you zoom, down to single samples, and the ruler stays readable on long clips.

**Also**
• Full screen hides the title bar, and the workspace fills the screen edge to edge. On Windows and Linux use **View → Toggle Full Screen** (**F11**)
• Dropping an instrument, effect, preset, sample or MIDI file selects the track it landed on
• A preset dropped on an instrument track loads into that track instead of adding a new one
• Sample-pack presets under Nota Mosaic → Packs expand in the browser again
• Nota Reverb no longer bursts into a loud, looping metallic ring after a few minutes; Nota Delay and the Wavetable chorus had the same rare bug and are fixed too
• VST3 plug-ins that change their inputs or outputs while running (Reaktor 6 switching modes) no longer crash Nota
• No more clicks when you turn Auto Filter, EQ-8 or Dynamic EQ-8 knobs fast
• The preset picker opens from anywhere on it and no longer shifts when the voice count changes
• The selected device card has a ring just outside its edge; panel gaps are tighter and the browser opens wider
• Plug-in search in Downloads no longer errors when you erase a search that found nothing
• The piano roll's GRID section is now **QUANTIZE** with its own step (1/16 by default), separate from the snap grid
